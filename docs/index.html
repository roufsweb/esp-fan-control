Hardware Wiring & Pinout

Standard Intel 4-Wire PWM laptop cooling fan specification:

Wire Color Signal ESP32-C3 Pin Configuration
Blue PWM Control GPIO 4 LEDC 25 kHz target frequency (eliminates coil whine).
Yellow Tachometer GPIO 3 Open-collector (2 pulses/rev). Internal pull-up enabled.
Red Power ($V_{cc}$) +5V / +12V Rail Power directly from fan-rated supply rail.
Black Ground GND Common reference ground with ESP32.

Getting Started Guide

  1. Flash Firmware: Use the Web Flasher above or the built-in USB-OTG Flasher inside the Android app.
  2. Install APK: Open the downloaded app-debug.apk on your Android device (Samsung Note 9 or any Android 8.0+ phone).
  3. Instant Connect: Launch the app with Bluetooth turned on. The app auto-discovers and connects to ESP32-C3 Fan in under 500ms.
  4. Control Modes: Use the 0–100% slider or select quick presets (Silent 25%, Balanced 50%, Boost 100%, Auto).
  5. Notification Controls: Access quick fan toggles directly from your lockscreen and Android Notification Shade.
